Emotional Intelligence Test: My Score and brief opinion

I took the emotional intelligences test on this website and got a score of 70 out of 100, which the page states to be above average.

I consider empathy to be one (if not the most) essential but also challenging features an emotionally smart person should have, as it implies a constant sense of listening to others' not only words, but also what's beyond it: the 'meta-language'. This concept differs from non-verbality as the former is even more complex to describe pragmatically compared to the latter. That's why I believe effective communication is a tool that help bridge the gap between what can not be communicated and what it can.
