I watched the video that summarizes the steps of a business
plan: [Plan de negocios – cómo hacer un plan de negocios en menos de un minuto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-KhVuakkl3g).

**Reflection:** the video condenses the process into eight steps in under a minute. That is useful as a checklist to see
the whole picture and not forget any section. But a plan is not only a list of sections. The hard part is validating
each assumption with real customers, and a one-minute video can't show that.

**Another video I
propose:** [Lean Canvas Workshop: Fast and effectively launch your business!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Nel66ZB9Ck&t=57s).
It presents the Lean
Canvas, a tool by Ash Maurya that summarizes an idea on a single page in about 20 minutes instead of writing a
traditional plan that takes weeks. I chose it because it answers the limitation I noticed above: the canvas treats its
content as assumptions to be tested, and it is easy to share with other people to get feedback.
