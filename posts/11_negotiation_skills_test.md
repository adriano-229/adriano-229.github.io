I took
the [negotiation skills test](https://www.psicologiahoy.com/co/test/carrera/test-habilidades-negociacion).
The score ranges are:

| Score     | Meaning                |
|-----------|------------------------|
| 0–18      | Very bad negotiations  |
| 19–36     | Bad negotiations       |
| 37–63     | Competent negotiations |
| **64–81** | **Good negotiations**  |
| 82–100    | Very good negotiations |

I scored **64**, which puts me in the Good negotiations range, right at its lower edge. I'm happy with the result,
but being at the border means there is still room to grow toward the top range.

**Reflection:** this fits with my leadership results. Being cooperative helps me find agreements, but I need to defend
my own interests more clearly, which is the same weak point I noticed in the leadership tests.
