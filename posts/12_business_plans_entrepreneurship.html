Videos on business plans and entrepreneurship

I watched the video that summarizes the steps of a business plan: Plan de negocios – cómo hacer un plan de negocios en menos de un minuto.

The video condenses the process into eight steps in under a minute. That is useful as a checklist to see the whole picture and not forget any section. But a plan is not only a list of sections. The hard part is validating each assumption with real customers, and a one-minute video can't show that.

Another video I propose: Lean Canvas Workshop: Fast and effectively launch your business!. It presents the Lean Canvas, a tool by Ash Maurya that summarizes an idea on a single page in about 20 minutes instead of writing a traditional plan that takes weeks. I chose it because it answers the limitation I noticed above.
