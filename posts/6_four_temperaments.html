Test on the 4 Temperaments

Complete the test by assigning a number from 1 to 4 to each horizontal row. Assign 4 to the word you identify with the most and 1 to the word you identify with the least. Each word in a row must receive a number from 1 to 4, and no numbers may be repeated within a row.

Once all rows are complete, add up the scores in each vertical column. The sum of the scores across all four columns must be 200. Otherwise, there is an error, and the entire test should be reviewed.

The highest-scoring columns determine which temperament or temperaments dominate your life.
