  • Answers 1–2: 4

Two Key Characteristics

1. Curiosity

I am interested in understanding how technology evolves and how it affects different professions. As a computer science student, I recognize the importance of continuous learning and adapting my knowledge to new tools and developments.

2. Openness, with room for better planning.

I am comfortable exploring new technologies and considering how artificial intelligence may transform the job market. However, I could improve by creating more concrete plans to prepare for these changes and by discussing their implications more frequently with others.

Conclusion

My results suggest that I have a positive attitude toward change and new technologies. Nevertheless, this doesn't mean I consider them all to be a possitve evolution, advances in tech may make us unwiser.
