Companies' Mission, Vision and Values, Stanley

Stanley 1913

Stanley 1913, founded by William Stanley Jr. in 1913, is a global lifestyle brand famous for inventing the all-steel vacuum bottle. For over a century, it has delivered durable, innovative food and beverage gear.

Vision

The company's vision is to "fuel the human experience, showing up for people who show up every day—across every continent, all around the world".

Mission

Stanley's mission is to "create, build and invent in a better way, for a better life and world". This is rooted in its "Built For Life™" promise, which drives its commitment to sustainability and reducing single-use waste.

Values

Stanley's core values are durability, relevance, and tradition—a commitment to products that last a lifetime, backed by a lifetime warranty. Parent company PMI adds guiding principles of sustainability, community, teamwork, accountability, and integrity.

Personal Opinion

What draws me to Stanley is its unwavering focus on quality and durability—values that feel increasingly overlooked today. In a culture where everything is treated as disposable, I am interested in products built to last, not to be replaced.

The company's logo is a crowned bear with wings. The bear represents resilience, the wings the nature or unbelievable, and the crown is a nod to the creator, who was a famous electrical engineer.
